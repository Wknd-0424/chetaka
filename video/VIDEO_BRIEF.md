# Chetaka — 30-second promo video brief

**Format:** 16:9 landscape, 1080p (also export 9:16 vertical for Instagram/Shorts)
**Length:** 30 seconds, 6 shots
**Tone:** tense first half, relief second half
**Colour story:** warm amber (home, trust) → cold blue (scammer) → red (danger) → calm green (protected)
**Voice:** calm, warm Indian English, female, slightly slow (~140 words per minute)
**Music:** low tense pulse for 0-12 s, drop to silence at "Until now", soft hopeful synth from 13 s

> Rule for all UI shots: invent a generic payment app. Do not show PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm,
> or bank logos — trademarks. Say "UPI app" in visuals.

---

## Shot list

| # | Time | Shot | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-5 s | Amma on phone | "Every day, parents like Amma get a call from 'the police'." |
| 2 | 5-9 s | Scammer | "Digital arrest. Pay now, or be jailed." |
| 3 | 9-16 s | Chetaka in-call alert | "Until now. Chetaka listens on the phone, in Telugu, Hindi and English, and warns mid-call." |
| 4 | 16-22 s | Payment app paused | "And if she opens a payment app after the call, Chetaka stops the payment first." |
| 5 | 22-26 s | Son gets alert | "Her son knows in seconds." |
| 6 | 26-30 s | End card | "Chetaka. The scam shield that follows the call into the payment app. Nothing leaves the phone." |

Word count: about 70 words, which fits 30 seconds at a calm pace.

## Refined image/video prompts

### Shot 1 — Amma on the phone (0-5 s)

```text
Cinematic medium close-up of a 65-year-old South Indian woman in a cotton saree, silver hair tied
back, reading glasses on a chain, sitting on a wooden sofa in a modest Hyderabad living room.
She holds a smartphone to her ear, eyebrows raised, lips parted, rising fear on her face.
Warm evening tungsten light from a side lamp, soft window light, brass lamp and family photo
frames blurred in background. Shallow depth of field, 35mm lens, subtle handheld movement,
slow push-in toward her face. Photorealistic, natural skin texture, film grain, 16:9, 4K.
```

Negative: cartoon, plastic skin, extra fingers, distorted hands, text, watermark, logos.

### Shot 2 — The scammer (5-9 s)

```text
Dark, cinematic shot of a man in silhouette speaking into a phone, face hidden in shadow,
wearing a collared shirt with a fake police-style ID card clipped on, in a cramped room.
Background: cold blue monitor glow, several phones on a desk, a printed fake "Cyber Crime"
poster slightly out of focus. Hard rim light from behind, heavy shadows, thin haze in the air.
Slow dolly left. Menacing, tense, photorealistic, anamorphic look, 16:9, 4K.
```

Negative: visible face, real police insignia, readable real logos, cartoon, gore.

### Shot 3 — Chetaka in-call alert (9-16 s)

```text
Close-up of a modern flagship Android smartphone held in an elderly woman's hand, screen facing
camera. The screen shows an active phone call with "Unknown number" at the top. A full-screen red
alert slides down over the call: bold white text "SCAM DETECTED", a shield icon, and a smaller
line "Police never ask for OTP. Hang up." Two large rounded buttons below: "Hang up" and
"Call family". A live risk meter fills from amber to red. The phone vibrates, shown by faint
motion rings around the edges. Clean, minimal, rounded Material-style UI, high contrast,
large readable text. Shallow depth of field, soft warm room light, 16:9, 4K.
```

Negative: misspelled text, garbled UI, real app logos, brand names.

### Shot 4 — Payment app paused (16-22 s)

```text
Over-the-shoulder shot of an elderly woman's thumb about to tap "Pay" in a generic UPI payment
app on an Android smartphone, amount "₹ 2,50,000" visible. Suddenly a calm deep-red overlay
covers the screen with a pause icon and a 30-second countdown ring. Headline text: "Wait. Are
they asking you to pay?" Subtext: "You just spoke to an unknown caller for 12 minutes. Real
police and banks never ask for money." Three buttons: "Cancel", "Call family", "Continue".
Her thumb stops mid-air. Modern clean UI, large text, generic app with no real logos,
soft background blur, warm light, 16:9, 4K.
```

Negative: PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, bank logos, garbled text.

### Shot 5 — Son receives the alert (22-26 s)

```text
Cinematic medium shot of a 30-year-old Indian man in a formal shirt at a modern office desk in
Bengaluru, evening, city lights through glass behind him. His phone lights up with a notification
card: "Chetaka Family Alert — Amma spoke to an unknown caller for 12 min and just opened a
payment app." His expression shifts from surprise to urgency; he stands and presses call.
Cool office light mixed with warm screen glow on his face. Shallow depth of field, 50mm lens,
photorealistic, 16:9, 4K.
```

Negative: cartoon, distorted hands, unreadable notification text.

### Shot 6 — End card (26-30 s)

```text
Minimal dark background with a soft green glow. A simple shield logo forms from a sound wave,
then the word "Chetaka" appears in clean modern type. Below it: "The scam shield that follows the
call into the payment app." Small line at the bottom: "On-device AI · Nothing leaves the phone".
Subtle particle motion, premium tech feel, 16:9, 4K.
```

## On-screen captions (burn in)

Burn in all voiceover lines as captions: large white text, dark shadow, bottom-centre.
Many judges will watch muted.
